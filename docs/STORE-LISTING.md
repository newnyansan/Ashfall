## Full description (max 4000)
Ten years ago the Ember King died, and his crown burst into five burning Embers that tore a wound in the sky. Ash has fallen ever since.

Ashfall is a single-player pixel-art RPG about one wanderer walking a ruined world: choosing a side, making friends and enemies, and deciding what kind of hero (or not) to be.

EVERY RUN IS DIFFERENT
• A new map every game, and 200+ hand-written events that shuffle people, places and gods, so no two journeys read alike
• Nine story arcs after the main quest. The story keeps going after you win
• People you met come back: the deserter you hid, the smith's lost daughter, the rival who keeps stealing your kills

FAIR AND FREE
• No ads, no energy timers, no loot boxes, no pay-to-win. The whole game is free
• An optional one-time Supporter Pack adds looks only: 9 hero outfits, colour themes, an ember trail and a 🔥 badge

BUILD YOUR HERO
• Nine weapon types, plus rings, necklaces and relics with random enchantments
• Seven classes taught by seven guild halls. Pass a guild trial to change class, or take a second class at level 10
• Ultimate abilities, skill trees, and a "best for your build" hint on every item
• Ascend items to legendary, reforge old favourites, and craft from ore you mine yourself

A LIVING WORLD
• Day and night, ash storms, eclipses, plagues, festivals and raider invasions
• Three factions at war: they besiege towns, and you can break the siege or join it
• Companions with their own stories. Earn their loyalty and they swear themselves to you
• Three wild outer lands: the Frostmarch, the Saltwaste and the Cinderlands, each with its own monsters
• The endless Ash Tower: climb as high as you dare
• Waystones, a homestead you can upgrade, a bestiary, a lore journal and achievements
• Built-in tips for your first steps, so new players always know where to go next

FOUR DIFFICULTIES
• Story, Normal, Hard, and one-life Ironman

PLAYS ANYWHERE
• Fully offline after the first launch
• No ads, no tracking, no account
• Three save slots, plus save codes to back up or move your game

Pixel art and sound by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0.
