Ashfall privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Ashfall is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device.

  • No account, no sign-in. The game does not ask for your name, email or any personal details. The hero name you type stays on your device.
  • Your saves stay on your device. Save slots are stored in your browser's local storage on your phone. They are never uploaded. Save codes you copy are created on your device and only go where you paste them.
  • No tracking. Ashfall has no analytics, no ads and no third-party trackers, and it does not collect or share any data.
  • Offline. After the first launch the game works without an internet connection.

If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, your saves are deleted.

Purchases. The optional Supporter Pack is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; Ashfall never sees your card or payment details. The game only stores, on your device, whether the pack is unlocked, and asks Google Play to confirm it when you tap Restore purchase.

Questions: contact the developer at the email address shown on the Google Play listing.
